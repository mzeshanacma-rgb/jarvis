# Privacy notice: outline (do not use before legal review)

This is not legal advice. It describes what the app does, so a lawyer and each school can turn it into a real notice.

## What data

| Data | Whose | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, username, email (optional), age group | Students, teachers, admins | Accounts and school work | While the account exists; anonymised on deletion |
| Answers (typed text, file, photo of a copy) | Students | Teacher's marking and feedback | The school's retention period (default 365 days), then deleted automatically |
| Writing record (time taken, size of pastes) | Students | Shows the teacher how work was written; makes no decision | With the answer |
| AI draft, teacher's scores, feedback | Students | Results | With the answer |
| Audit log | Everyone | Who viewed or changed what | 3 years |

## What goes to the AI (Claude)

- Task instructions, rubric, sample answers.
- The student's answer. For photos, the top of the page (name, roll number) is cropped on the device first. A name written inside the answer itself is not removed.
- A code instead of the name (for example `S-7K2QX`).
- AI processing is done by Anthropic (Claude API) in the "global" or "us" region. The database and files are on our server in ____.
- Student data is not used to train any AI model. (Confirm in writing against Anthropic's commercial terms.)

## What the app does not do

- No camera, face or emotion monitoring.
- No "AI detector" score.
- No ads, profiling or selling data.
- Accounts for under-18s are created only by the school; there is no open sign-up.

## Rights

- At the school's request, all of a student's data is deleted and a deletion proof code is produced.
- Students can ask for a re-check of a result.
